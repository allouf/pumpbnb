## ⚠️ **IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS:**

### **"Create Coin" Button:**
- ✅ Creates token metadata
- ✅ Uploads images and description
- ✅ Reserves ticker name
- ❌ Does NOT deploy to blockchain
- ❌ Does NOT make token tradeable
- 💰 **Cost**: FREE

### **"Start Livestream" Button:**
- ✅ Actually deploys token to Solana blockchain
- ✅ Makes token live and tradeable
- ✅ Charges the creation fee
- ✅ Token becomes searchable and buyable
- 💰 **Cost**: ~0.02 SOL
