## 🛠️ Quick Resize Methods:

### **Option 1: Online Resizer (EASIEST)**

**Step 1: Go to ResizeImage.net**
1. Visit: **https://resizeimage.net**
2. Click "Choose Images"
3. Upload your `OIG1.jfif` file

**Step 2: Resize to 1000x1000**
1. Set Width: `1000`
2. Set Height: `1000` 
3. Choose "Ignore Aspect Ratio" (to make it square)
4. Click "Resize Images"
5. Download as `CRAZY_main_1000x1000.png`

---

### **Option 2: Using Windows Paint (FREE)**

**Step 1: Open in Paint**
1. Right-click your image
2. "Open with" → "Paint"

**Step 2: Resize**
1. Click "Resize" button (or Ctrl+W)
2. Select "Pixels" 
3. Uncheck "Maintain aspect ratio"
4. Set Horizontal: `1000`
5. Set Vertical: `1000`
6. Click "OK"

**Step 3: Save**
1. File → "Save as" → "PNG picture"
2. Name: `CRAZY_main_1000x1000.png`

---

### **Option 3: Canva (BEST QUALITY)**

**Step 1: Go to Canva**
1. Visit: **https://canva.com**
2. Create free account if needed
3. Click "Create a design"
4. Choose "Custom size": 1000 x 1000 px

**Step 2: Upload Your Image**
1. Click "Uploads" in left panel
2. Upload your `OIG1.jfif`
3. Drag it onto the 1000x1000 canvas
4. Stretch to fill entire canvas

**Step 3: Download**
1. Click "Share" → "Download"
2. Choose PNG format
3. Download as `CRAZY_main_1000x1000.png`
